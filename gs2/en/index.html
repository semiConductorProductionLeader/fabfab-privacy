Build your own chip fab

Place machines, hire workers and grow a small warehouse into a chip company.

The sequel to Growing Semiconductors. This time you build the factory floor yourself: where you place each machine shapes every worker's walk.

A factory you lay out

Place every machine on the grid yourself. Far-apart steps mean long walks; close ones mean faster work. See what holds you back, then rearrange it or take the suggested layout.

Things go wrong on the floor

Overloads, sleepy workers, contamination, drifting machines, empty chemical tanks. Tap to fix, swipe to fix many at once, or buy automation to fix them for you.

Make chips, fill orders

From RAM and CPUs to smartphones, GPUs, servers, AI chips, car chips, VR headsets and robots. Fab 1 through Fab 5 each make their own chips.

Runs while you're away

Close the app and production keeps piling up for up to 8 hours. When you return, you see what you earned. Power saver mode dims the screen and keeps the factory running.

Workers who grow

Workers who level up and elite workers, achievements and daily rewards, and a lucky drone that drops by. Screens and music change from day to night.

Rankings and cloud backup

Compare your factory's power with other companies. Carry on on a new phone with a single backup code.

Account and Data Deletion

How to delete the backup, ranking entry and backup code that 'Growing Semiconductors 2' (developer: Factory Jam 작업반장 · FactoryJam) keeps on its server. The game has no sign-up: the first time it reaches the server, it creates an account automatically under a 12-character backup code.

1. Delete in the game

  • Open the game and go to Menu → Settings.
  • Press 'Delete' next to 'Delete backup and ranking data', then 'Yes' in the window that asks.
  • Your backup, ranking entry, company name and backup code (account) are deleted from the server at once.

2. Ask by email without the app

If you have already removed the app or lost your device, email us your backup code (XXXX-XXXX-XXXX, shown in Settings) or your company name. We delete the data within 7 days of confirming it and reply.

Email: rlaxodn5235@gmail.com

3. What is deleted and what stays

  • Deleted: the backup on the server (the save, including company name, logo, progress and purchase records), the ranking entry (company name, logo number, power score), the company name reservation and the backup code account. No other copies are kept.
  • Stays: your progress on the device is untouched (it is removed when you uninstall the app). If you keep playing after deleting, the game backs up again under a new backup code.
  • Purchase records (account number, product, order number, time) stay for 1 year from the day they are recorded, to check for fraudulent purchases, and are deleted after that.
  • For a suspended account, the account and its entry on the suspension list stay until the suspension is lifted (the backup, ranking entry and company name are deleted).
  • Store payment records are kept by Google Play and the App Store under their own policies.

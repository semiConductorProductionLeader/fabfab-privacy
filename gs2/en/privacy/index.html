Privacy Policy

1. Purpose of Processing Personal Information

'Growing Semiconductors 2' is a game that can be played without creating an account. Game progress is stored on your device. To let you get your progress back when you lose or change devices (cloud backup), and to let companies compare their power (ranking), the information in section 2 is stored on a server. Purchase records and a suspension list are also stored on the server, to detect fraudulent purchases and use and to suspend the accounts involved.

2. Information Processed

  • Cloud backup: your game progress (the save, including your company name, logo, progress and purchase records). It is sent every 10 minutes while you play, when you close the app or switch to another app, and when you press 'Back up now' in Settings.
  • Ranking: your company name, logo number and power score. The ranking list is visible to other players.
  • Backup code: a 12-character code the game makes the first time it reaches the server (for a backup, the ranking or checking a company name). It is used only as the key to find your backup and ranking data. The server keeps only a value from which the code cannot be read. Anyone who has the code can load that backup, so do not share it.
  • Purchase records: your account number (the number of the server account made with your backup code), the product, the store (Google Play or App Store), the order number given by the store, the time of the record, the result of checking the store's signature, and refund notices. They are sent when you make a purchase and when the store notifies the app of a refund. If the account on your device changes ('Delete backup and ranking data', 'Load a backup by code'), the previous account number is sent too.
  • Suspension list: the numbers of suspended accounts. It is filled in by the operator; the game only checks whether its own account is on it, when it starts and when you return to the app.
  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, email or any other contact details.

3. Processing by a Service Provider and Transfer Abroad

Backups, rankings, purchase records and the suspension list are stored with Google's Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Firebase Authentication).

  • Recipient: Google LLC
  • Information transferred: the information in section 2, and access records created automatically while using the service (such as IP addresses)
  • Location: game data is stored in the Republic of Korea (Seoul region); account sign-in data may be processed on Google's servers, including in the United States.
  • When and how: each time a backup, ranking entry or purchase record is sent, over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Retention: as in section 7.
  • Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Information Collection by Third-Party Services

This app includes the following third-party services (SDKs) to show advertisements and to process in-app purchases. These service providers may collect and process device information, advertising IDs and other data in order to deliver their services.

  • Google AdMob (Advertising)

Data collected: Ad data, device identifiers (Advertising ID), app usage history, crash logs

Purpose: Serving ads, including personalized ads, ad performance analysis, fraud prevention

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

  • Google Play Services (Android core services)

Data collected: Device information, log data

Purpose: App security, updates, and essential features such as in-app purchases

5. Advertising Consent

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland see an ad consent form on first launch and can change their choices at any time under 'Settings → Privacy options' in the game.

On iOS the app does not ask for permission to track, so ads do not use the device's advertising identifier (IDFA).

6. In-App Purchases

This app offers in-app purchases through Google Play and the Apple App Store.

  • Payment Processing: All payments are processed by the respective store. The developer does not directly collect or store any payment information such as credit card details.
  • Purchase Records: So that a purchase is never granted twice, the transaction number given by the store is saved in the game data on your device. Purchase history is linked to your store account, and one-time products (the Starter Pack) can be restored via 'Shop → Restore purchases' in the app.
  • Purchase Records and Fraud Checks: a purchase record (section 2) is kept on the server for every purchase. Google Play purchases are granted only when the store's signature is valid. The operator compares the records with the stores' order history to find accounts that try fake purchases or keep using items after a refund, and may suspend them.
  • Refunds: Cancellations and refunds follow each store's policies and this game's Refund Policy.

7. Retention and Deletion of Data

  • Data on your device: uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it immediately.
  • Backup, ranking entry and backup code on the server: kept until you delete them. 'Settings → Delete backup and ranking data' in the game deletes your backup, ranking entry and backup code from the server at once. If you keep playing after that, a new backup code is made and your backup and ranking entry go up again. If you have already removed the app or lost your device, email us your backup code or company name and we will delete them.
  • Purchase records: kept for 1 year from the day they are recorded; the records of a month are deleted in the month after they turn 1 year old. They stay when you use 'Delete backup and ranking data' (to check for fraudulent purchases).
  • Suspension list: kept until the suspension is lifted. If a suspended account asks for deletion, the account (a value from which the backup code cannot be read) and its entry on the suspension list are kept until the suspension is lifted (to prevent misuse); the backup, ranking entry and company name are deleted.

8. Your Rights

You can view, correct or delete your information on the server at any time. You can see your backup with 'Settings → Load backup' and your ranking entry in the ranking window, change your company name in Settings, and delete your data as described in section 7.
